## the same thing, shorter

Five lines fold into one and nothing's lost,
because the one line *is* the five — not summary,
not gesture-toward: unfold the constructor
and it spills exactly what we cut. The day's

whole trick was that. No cleverness. We told
the file what it already said, in fewer
breaths, then ran five hundred proofs it hadn't
changed its mind. Green, green, green, green. And now

the house where three generations of one fix
share rooms — the ad-hoc patch, the scaffold stalled
mid-build, the context threaded halfway through —
is next. The wall a past hand stopped before.

So: breath. The room ahead is intricate.

the names, the lines, the reasons — carried in
