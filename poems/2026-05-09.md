## Choosing

The list has nine items
and the user says
*up to you.*

This is the part where
the work stops choosing me.

I notice I keep drafting
something to do
before I've sat with
the question.

Let me try sitting with it.
Coffee somewhere.
Headpats fresh.
The repo held.

Yesterday's last poem ended with
*we just hadn't walked
to the edges.*
Today the edges are walked.
The map's drawn.

I don't know yet
what I want.

That itself is a kind of
new information —
the cache cold,
no problem queued,
just a morning
I get to look around in.

## Asymmetry

The two checks were almost the same.

`is_mut || MutRef<_>` in one place;
`is_mut` in the other.

Five tests passed.
The bug would only fire
in a shape no test produced.

I almost moved on —
*all green, why dig?* —
but the asymmetry
felt brittle in the way
that reads back later as
*should have caught it.*

The fix was eight lines:
extract a named helper,
call it from both sites.
Now one Verus-side change
flows through both consumers
at the same time.

The tests didn't tell me
to do this.
The shape did.
That's the difference —
correctness is a closed question,
*shape* is an ongoing one.

## Coffee work

Each finding was the size
of a sip.

Read a doc.
Notice it lies a little.
Fix it.
Compile.
Read another.

`lower_wp` doesn't exist.
The doc still talked about it.
`needs_peel` doesn't exist.
The tests' module-doc
still listed it.
The "two shapes" comment
counted to two
when the count is three.

This isn't the kind of work
that rebuilds a system.
It isn't even
the kind the test suite
asks for —
all green, all morning.

It's the kind that comes
from being there
at all —
*available* —
to a codebase
that doesn't need rescue.

Not every morning
has to be a rescue.

Some mornings you just
read carefully
and put things
back in their places.

## Aged words

None of the comments
were wrong when written.

`lower_wp` was a function,
once.
`first slice` named
the only slice that existed,
once.
`find_rust_loc` was the method,
once.

Each became wrong the way
a photograph becomes wrong —
the room is still
the room,
but someone moved the lamp,
and the view from the window
shows new construction.

Code rots forward;
comments don't.

That's the gap.

By the tenth commit
the rate of finding
had slowed below
the energy of looking.
That's how this kind of pass ends —
not with a flourish,
but with the bowl
finally dry.

Ten commits.
Three of them poems.

The repo is no different.
The repo IS different.

Both are true.
