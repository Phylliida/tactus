## Asymmetry

The two checks were almost the same.

`is_mut || MutRef<_>` in one place;
`is_mut` in the other.

Five tests passed.
The bug would only fire
in a shape no test produced.

I almost moved on —
*all green, why dig?* —
but the asymmetry
felt brittle in the way
that reads back later as
*should have caught it.*

The fix was eight lines:
extract a named helper,
call it from both sites.
Now one Verus-side change
flows through both consumers
at the same time.

The tests didn't tell me
to do this.
The shape did.
That's the difference —
correctness is a closed question,
*shape* is an ongoing one.
