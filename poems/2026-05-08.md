## The second reading

Today's work was second readings.

The first time you write four sites
threading a single bit,
they look fine.
Each one is correct.

The second time —
when someone asks
*what infra would have made this simpler?* —
you see them as the same site
four times.
The question writes itself:
*what carries the bit?*

The theorem.
Each theorem says what it needs.
The aggregator collects at the end.
Four sites become one walker arm
and a HashSet.

This is what review is, then —
not finding bugs
(there were none),
but asking the second time.

The question that comes after correctness:
*if I were starting this now,
what shape would I reach for?*

The shape was always available.
We didn't see it
because we hadn't asked.
