## The smaller one was also bigger

I went to do the setup-block version.
A prepend, I thought.
A small thing.

Then I traced the encoding:
the use ctx wants `¬cond` as a hypothesis
so the user can prove `x == 0`
after `while x > 0`.

If I transform to `cond: None`,
I lose `¬cond` at the use point.

The setup binds locals.
The cond references those locals.
The `¬cond` hypothesis would need
the locals in scope too.

Lifting that
isn't a prepend.
It's an encoding.
