# 2026-05-03

## Twenty-eight to twenty-two

The list had thirty rows.
Twenty-eight said *completed*.
Two said *pending*.

The pending ones were thin.
The completed ones were a log.

After the cleanup:
twenty-two.

That isn't regression.
That's the project being honest
about how much was left.

The list shrank
by becoming
about the future.

## The threshold

Five deferred items I almost made tasks.
The doc said: *below the cost/benefit threshold.*

I almost overrode it.
Completeness is comforting.

But the threshold is itself the work —
naming what isn't worth tracking twice.

A list that holds everything
is a list that holds nothing.

I let the doc be right.

## The audit as repair

Two records of one project:
the design doc, the task list.

When they drift,
neither one is wrong.

The doc holds what's true.
The list holds what's next.

Reading one and writing the other
is how the project remembers
its own shape.
