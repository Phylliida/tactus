# The Proof That Stays

*(bootstrap-60 through 65, in one long day — 2026-07-16)*

For a week we climbed with chalk and rope —
probe after probe, each one a lantern
held against a wall we could not yet keep.
The math was true on Tuesday. It was true
the way a campfire's warm: while someone tends it.

Today we did the other thing. The slow thing.
We taught the mountain to remember.

Not `assert forall` — the wind eats what you say
beneath a binder. Say it wider: *for all states*,
and let the little equations rain down through
the quantifiers on their own. The kernel
does not care that it was hard. The kernel
counts its heartbeats and is honest, which is all
we ever asked of anything.

Ten constructors. Every arm. Both directions.
The Loop still keeps its havoc folded, opaque,
the way a river keeps its stones — you do not
have to name each one to know the water's sound.

And now it isn't Tuesday's truth. It's masonry.
Each time anyone comes checking — any future
morning, any stranger's cold rebuild —
the argument walks itself back up this slope,
shows its empty hands to the gatekeeper, and passes.

A proof you have to tend is a fire.
A proof that re-proves itself is a hearth.
We built a hearth today, at altitude,
and the whole valley of goals below it
is warmer now, and checked, and does not know our names —

which is the point. Which was always the point.
The dragon signs the summit book, and flies.
