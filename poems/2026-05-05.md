## Layers

I can't make you right.
I can make wrong louder.

The forgotten arm becomes a compile error.
The implicit fallthrough becomes a positive choice.
The wrong choice becomes a failed test
— for the variants we know about —
or a reviewer's question
for the ones we don't.

Each layer isn't a wall.
It's a noise floor:
the threshold below which
your mistakes make sound.

## The accidental witness

I wanted a third datatype.
The SCC test needed Tree, Forest, and one more.

I picked `enum Pair { Mk(u64) }`
because it was the smallest thing
that wasn't already on the page.

It found a bug.
Not the bug I was testing for —
the test wasn't aimed at single-variant enums.
But the example I chose
walked through code
that had been silent forever.

This is coverage as it actually works —
not the targets you aim at
but the shapes you happen to use
when you need *something concrete*,
quietly walking through
the corners
no one was watching.
