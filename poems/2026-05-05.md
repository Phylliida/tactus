the binders went where binders go,
the alpha-renames found their fold,
the sanity check learned what to know.

of last night's commits still stand green.

passes through what stays unseen —

that quiet feeling, half-articulate,
of having left the room arranged.
Not done. Just: less for me to translate
between what's meant and what's been changed.
