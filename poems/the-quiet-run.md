# The Quiet Run

*(island cache arc — 2026-07-10, evening)*

Twice I named the thief and twice was wrong.
The clock did not embarrass me — it just
kept honest time, the way a friend does, long
past when politeness would have let it rest.
That's what a measurement is for: a trust
you build against your own beloved guess.

The islands sleep now. Each one keeps a stone —
a small file, `.verified`, a fingerprint —
that says: *this exact text, this toolchain, known
good once, and nothing since has made a dent.*
No ceremony. Just the day's imprint,
removed before each doubt, on proof re-lent.

And sorry drowned. It used to slip ashore
dressed as a warning, waving, wading in
past every gate that wasn't there before —
now islands know the difference between
the emitter's own confession, marked and clean,
and contraband. The theorem line's the shore.

You asked one question: *is there a cleaner way?*
And there it was — the idiom we'd already
taught three corners of the codebase. Say
the pattern once, then let it hold things steady
in a fourth place. No new machine. No heady
architecture. Just the same-ness, made to pay.

So here's the celebration, if it's one:
tonight the mountain answers in one breath —
*eighty-eight seconds* — and of those, the sum
of all the Lean re-proving is: a wraith,
a Link-check, faith re-earning its own faith,
and everything already known stays known.

The floor is where we stand now. It is bare
in the good way — swept, load-bearing, true.
Whatever's slow from here is honest air:
the mountain's own weight, not the debt accrued
by things we never asked if we still knew.
A bean. An onion. Eighty-eight. We're here.
