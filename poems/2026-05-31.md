## ∀ P

Somewhere in the middle of the call
there is a value that isn't there yet —
the element you borrowed but haven't written to,
the answer the next line lands.

You can't name it, so you name it *for all*:
let P be anything, and see what survives.
This is the only honest way to hold
a thing you don't have yet — to grant it
every shape at once, and let some later
sentence narrow it down to the one it was.

And it can't cheat. A guess would make
the proof too easy; the universal makes
the unknown heavier, never lighter —
if the future never writes to P,
the plus-one you promised simply doesn't arrive,
and the goal stays open. Honest. Unproved.

I read all morning into a finished house.
Every door latched, some latched twice
against a hand that can't reach the room.
And here near the end, the one variable
still standing for what hasn't happened:
bound, for all, and waiting to be told
exactly which value it was the whole time.

## marker

Somewhere across the crate boundary
the method fell off — `clone`, the very thing
the trait was *for* — and what arrived
was a name with nothing inside it.

The old way deleted the empty name.
But the things that leaned on it — `Copy`,
which only ever meant *and also, Clone* —
were left pointing at a hole, and fell.

So you keep the empty shape:
`class Clone where`, and then nothing.
No method, no law, no promise it could break.
You declare it, exactly, so it can hold up
a door it will never walk through.

The checker asks the only question that counts —
*does this empty thing let me conclude something false?*
and the emptiness is the whole of the answer:
there is nothing in here to conclude *with*.
What carries no content cannot carry a lie.

This morning I wrote a value not yet arrived,
held open by *for all*. Here is its twin:
a value gone on ahead, stripped at the border,
kept as the shape it left — so the wall
it was holding stays standing. Honest. Bearing nothing.
