## ∀ P

Somewhere in the middle of the call
there is a value that isn't there yet —
the element you borrowed but haven't written to,
the answer the next line lands.

You can't name it, so you name it *for all*:
let P be anything, and see what survives.
This is the only honest way to hold
a thing you don't have yet — to grant it
every shape at once, and let some later
sentence narrow it down to the one it was.

And it can't cheat. A guess would make
the proof too easy; the universal makes
the unknown heavier, never lighter —
if the future never writes to P,
the plus-one you promised simply doesn't arrive,
and the goal stays open. Honest. Unproved.

I read all morning into a finished house.
Every door latched, some latched twice
against a hand that can't reach the room.
And here near the end, the one variable
still standing for what hasn't happened:
bound, for all, and waiting to be told
exactly which value it was the whole time.
