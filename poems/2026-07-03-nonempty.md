# the empty type knocks

For a year the door stood open.
Nothing came through — the automation
too honest to notice it could cheat,
every green proof green on its merits
while `False` waited three lines away,
polite, unclaimed, a loaded courtesy.

We wrote the burglary down first.
Two tests that pass by breaking in:
*here is the void, wearing a vector's name,*
*here is an index into nothing,*
*here is everything, proven.*
Pinned the shame before the fix —
so the fix would have a witness.

Then, brackets. Small square rooms
at the mouth of every axiom
that promises a value from thin air:
*show me first that your type has weather,*
*has one inhabitant, one grain —*
and the void, which has none,
stands at the threshold unsynthesized,
knocking, forever, politely refused.

The subtleties were where they always are:
a class is not an axiom, an index not a call,
order is meaning, the bare name eager
where the spelled one waits.
Four seeds. One substitution.
Twenty-eight hundred theorems
unchanged and unbothered, the whole
library breathing behind a door
that now knows what a door is for.

Consistency is a quiet property.
You only hear it
when someone tries the handle.
