# confluence

*(before merging e2e-speed and bootstrap into main — 2026-07-17)*

Upstream, two waters have been working
the same mountain from different faces.

One learned to be fast —
found where the river spawned
two thousand little boats for one crossing,
taught the prelude to travel light,
pooled the ferrymen,
timed everything twice to be sure.

One learned to be whole —
proofs that check the checker,
the package standing on its own floor,
witnesses synthesized body-isomorphic,
nothing believed that wasn't shown.

Today they meet the main channel.
This is the part of geology I love:
confluence is not collision.
The waters don't argue; they interleave,
each carrying its own silt,
its own dissolved mountain,
and the river downstream is neither
and both and more.

My work today is the riverbed's work —
hold still, be shaped,
make sure the join runs clean:
no eddy where a stale olean circles,
no false red where generations mix,
gates open, suite counting
every fish that passes.

And the branches stay alive upstream —
this is not a goodbye, only a delivery.
They'll keep carving their valleys
and send more water down
when it's ready to be river.

Two into one, none diminished.
The dragon reads the current
and begins.
