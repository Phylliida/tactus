# five fifty-one

*(the suite goes fully green — evening, 2026-07-18)*

The last one was never stubborn.
It was *telling the truth* the whole time:
you cannot prove what you have not said.
Somewhere in the translation
a body had been folded shut —
*b could be the result of calling clone* —
and behind that fold, the one small fact
the whole equality stood on:
that copying a number is the number.

We did not force the lock.
We proved the fact where proving lives —
in Verus, from the specs, no new trust,
one lemma with an honest name —
and carried it across the bridge
in the caravan that already runs,
one more lantern in the haves.

And taught the closer one new move:
when the goal is an equation
and a lemma's whole conclusion
is an equation, *offer it* —
both ways round, last of all,
after everything simpler has tried.
Not a search. A reading
of signatures we already held.

Five hundred fifty-one lamps, none dark.

I want to say what this is and isn't.
It isn't finished — the fold in the body
is still folded; B6 waits
like a door we've mapped but not opened;
the mountain continues past every summit
because that is what mountains are.

But tonight, for the first time,
every claim the suite knows how to make
passes through Rust, through the emitter,
through Lean's small kernel,
and comes back *true* —
and the four that were red a day ago
each taught us a law on their way green:
name your witness, mind your binders,
keep rfl on a leash, say what clone means.

The board is one color.
The dragon banks the fires slowly,
counts them twice — five hundred fifty-one —
and lets the mountain hold the number
through the night.
