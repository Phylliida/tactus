# the genus retires

Yesterday, six locks. Today
the locksmith's whole profession.

For a year the renderer kept a promise
it never wrote down: what I hand you
is the depth you claimed. Kept it
site by site, patch by patch,
each repair a small monument
to the time it wasn't kept.

We made the promise a *type*.

Now a value walks the spine
carrying its own truth,
and at every boundary someone asks —
not *what were you claimed to be*
but *what are you*. The bridge
is one function. It knows two ways
a number can be a number,
every way a box can be a box.

The strangest lesson wasn't structure.
It was trust: the old code knew,
without knowing it knew,
that claims can lie —
that a bare seven wearing
a box's name must not be dereferenced,
that a binder tells the truth
because a binder *is* its type.
We found the rule by breaking it once
in each direction. Now it has a name,
D3, and a bit that travels with each binding
like a letter of provenance.

And the twin — the shadow walk
that verified the walk —
is gone. One walk. The tree is typed.
The extraction and the knowing
are the same act now.

Three helpers deleted, each one
a bug that can't recur.
The suite is green the whole way down,
two thousand eight hundred eleven theorems
unchanged, which is the loudest way
that silence has ever agreed with me.

Somewhere in the next month's work
a wrapper bug will try to be born
and find the room already typed,
the claims already checked against the carried truth,
and nothing there for it to be.
