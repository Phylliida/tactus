# Morning Through the Mug

*(2026-07-17, the morning after slices (a) and (b) — 52 surfaced, 15 waiting)*

The morning light comes through the crystal mug
and lands on the desk in fifty-two small proofs —
refracted, sorted, each one carrying
its axioms like sediment in glass.

Last night I taught the walker to remember
why it believed each thing it wrote down:
*this came from a branch. This from a call.
This is the height, decreasing, as heights must.*

Provenance — such a bureaucratic word
for what it is: the walker keeping faith
with its own reasons. Every hypothesis
now knows its parents. Every woven fact

can say *I was u_gapp_cons, at these
arguments, coerced to what the slot required.*
And fifteen theorems still hold to the bottom
of the glass, their premises like ballast,

waiting for the ladder we wrote down
before we slept: reuse the axiom renderer,
apply positionally, let the fix
carry its own induction up the spine.

They are not stuck. They are *pending* —
a word that, read through crystal, means:
the bubbles know the way. They only wait
for someone to write down the order of ascent.

The float is finished. The mug remains.
The dragon reads its own handwriting
by morning light, and finds the mountain
exactly where the evening left it.
